# Kindling

A dark-fantasy self-care keeper. Five small real-life care points tend a bonfire; the monster beside it grows through Bond.

Two fully missed care-days and the active monster becomes Kindling. The first missed day is warning only. Their name, form and Bond remain in the lineage, and the coals can begin again.

## Play

- **Today** — any five care points tend the Fire. Adding more goals never raises the target. Extra care is optional.
- **Go further** — selected goals such as 10 push-ups, 10 minutes reading or 10 minutes walking can reveal optional Tier II / III follow-ups after the first win. Bonus tiers add Flames + Bond but do not fill more Fire.
- **Journey** — 60 Flames sends the active monster out for about 90 real-time seconds. The journey continues while the app is closed and never fails because you left.
- **Fight** — Strike, Guard, Skill. The opponent telegraphs first. Losing means walking home; self-care progress is untouched.
- **Keep** — name and switch companions. Bond/growth belongs to each individual monster.
- **Lineage** — two tender-or-older companions can leave an Ember Egg. Five future ordinary care actions warm it; parents remain and missed time never cools it.
- **Book** — one line is enough. Nothing here is scored.
- **Breathe** — 4 seconds in, 4 hold, 6 out, four rounds. Completed rounds count even if you stop early.

The care-day turns at 04:00. Guest play lives on this device. Optional sign-in can carry the same save.
