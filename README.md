# Kindling

A dark-fantasy keeper. Five small things tend a bonfire. A monster stays if you do.

Two fully missed care-days and they become kindling — not a scolding, a story. Their name stays in the lineage. The coals can still hatch another.

## Play

- **Today** — check a care, the fire climbs. Four is a good fire.
- **Walk** — three kindling sends them out. They bring something home, or they meet what lives there.
- **Fight** — Strike, Guard, Skill. The other side telegraphs first. You can keep who you beat.
- **Keep** — name them, switch who sits by the fire, pair two who can leave an egg in the coals.
- **Book** — one line is enough.

The day turns at 04:00. Guest play lives on this device. Sign in to carry it.
